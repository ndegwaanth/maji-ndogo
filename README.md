**Maji Ndogo** is a project aimed at addressing water management and distribution issues in a simulated urban environment. The project focuses on analyzing water resources, distribution networks, and consumption patterns to ensure efficient water management. It combines data analysis, simulation, and visualization techniques to provide insights into water usage and infrastructure.
